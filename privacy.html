Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Fernway collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Fernway LLC (“we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to orders you place with us.

Who we are

Fernway LLC, 1420 SE Cedar Mill Road, Suite 5, Portland, OR 97214, United States. For any privacy question, email hello@shopfernway.us.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Name & shipping addressTo process and ship your order
Email & phoneOrder confirmation and shipping updates
Order historyHandling returns, refunds and support
Email (newsletter)Sending seasonal tips and offers, only if you opt in
Inquiry messagesReplying to your questions

The newsletter is an unchecked option — you are never signed up automatically, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Cookies & analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar local storage:

  • Cart & preferences (essential) — local storage that remembers what is in your cart and that you have seen our cookie notice. It stays on your device.
  • Microsoft Clarity (analytics) — we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site (aggregate page views and clicks, plus heatmaps and anonymized session replays) so we can improve it. Clarity sets cookies (for example _clck and _clsk) and is provided by Microsoft as our processor. It does not tell us who you are, and Clarity masks the content you type in form fields by default.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data. Microsoft’s handling of Clarity data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement (microsoft.com/privacy), where you can also manage your privacy choices.

Who we share information with

We share only what is necessary, and only with companies that help us run the store:

  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order.
  • Our payment processor, to take payment securely. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Microsoft, as our analytics provider (Clarity), as described above.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where we are required by law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfill the order and then as required for tax and accounting purposes. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Inquiry messages are kept for up to two years.

Your choices & rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from marketing at any time. Residents of California and certain other states have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To make a request, email hello@shopfernway.us and we will verify and respond as required by law.

Children

This store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
