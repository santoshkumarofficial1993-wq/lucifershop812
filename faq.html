Frequently asked questions

Shipping, returns, plants and payment — the things people ask before they buy.

How much is shipping?

Standard U.S. shipping is $5.95 and takes 3–5 business days. It is free on orders over $75. Express shipping is $14.95 for 1–2 business days. Bulky items carry a $25 oversize handling charge, which is always shown in your cart before you pay. Full detail is on our shipping page.

Do prices include sales tax?

Prices are shown before tax, which is standard for U.S. stores. Where we are required to collect it, sales tax is calculated from your shipping address and shown as a separate line at checkout, so you always see the full total before you place your order.

Where do you ship?

We ship to all 50 states. Estimated delivery times apply to the contiguous U.S.; Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories take longer and may carry an extra carrier charge for bulky items, which we always confirm with you first. We do not currently ship outside the United States.

Can I return something if I change my mind?

Yes. You have 30 days from delivery to return most items for a refund. They should come back unused and in their original packaging. See the full returns & refunds policy for how return shipping works.

Are your plants safe for pets?

It depends on the plant. Several popular houseplants, including monstera and fiddle leaf fig, are toxic to cats and dogs if eaten. We state pet safety on every plant's product page so you can choose with confidence.

Will outdoor furniture survive winter?

Our outdoor furniture is chosen to live outside, and each product page tells you exactly how it behaves — for example, teak that weathers to gray, or aluminum frames that will not rust. Where something needs to come under cover or have its cushions stored, we say so plainly in the care notes.

How do I track my order?

You will get a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link when your order leaves us. If it has not updated for two business days, contact us and we will follow up.

Do you take payment securely?

This site is a demonstration storefront and does not process real payments or store card details. In a live shop, payment would be handled by a PCI-compliant payment processor over an encrypted connection.
