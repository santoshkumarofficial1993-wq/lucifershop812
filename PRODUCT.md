# Fernway — Product & Brand Brief

**What it is.** A US-based Home & Garden e-commerce storefront (Portland, OR). Static HTML/CSS/JS, no
backend; cart in `localStorage`, demo checkout. 110 products across outdoor furniture, planters, indoor
greenery, garden tools, outdoor lighting, and decor. Built to be usable as a Google Ads destination.

**Register.** Brand — the storefront's look and feel is part of the product. Premium but friendly,
retail-forward.

**Audience.** US home gardeners and homeowners furnishing yards, balconies and rooms; ranges from
first houseplant to a full patio set.

**Brand.**
- Name: **Fernway** — a coined, warm word evoking a green path; spans home + garden.
- Logo: a stroked fern-frond mark in a teal roundel + "Fernway" wordmark in Fraunces. `assets/favicon.svg`.
- Voice: plain, honest, unhurried. Describes how things behave outdoors, including what they *won't* do.
  No hype, no fake urgency, no invented reviews.

**Design system — "Botanic Teal".** Cool botanical base + warm coral pop on pure white. Teal `#0E6E63`
(brand/links/prices/in-stock), coral `#CB4A22` (CTAs, white text AA), brighter `#E9663F` decorative,
ink `#13211F`, mist `#EEF3F1` panels. Type: Fraunces display serif + Nunito Sans. All text AA-verified.
Full token list and rules in `RULES.md` §3.4.

**Hard constraints (see `RULES.md`).** Google Ads policy compliance; real downloaded photography only
(Unsplash License) in `assets/images/`, SVG for UI/logo only; prices in cents, pre-tax with sales tax
estimated at checkout; no third-party/AI branding anywhere; WCAG AA; no build step or runtime CDN.

**Pre-launch.** `BUSINESS-DETAILS.md` lists placeholder identity values (legal entity, address, phone,
`.example` emails) and the real sales-tax calculation to wire before advertising.
