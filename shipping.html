Shipping & Delivery

Where we ship, what it costs and how long it takes.

Shipping options & cost

OptionCostEstimated time
Standard shipping$5.953–5 business days
Express shipping$14.951–2 business days
Standard, orders over $75Free3–5 business days

A $25 oversize handling charge applies to bulky items (larger furniture, wheelbarrows and rugs). Where a charge applies it is clearly labeled in your cart and on the checkout page, so you always see the full cost before you pay.

Where we ship

We ship to all 50 states. Estimated times above apply to the contiguous United States. Delivery to Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories takes longer and, for bulky items, may carry an additional carrier charge. If that applies to your order we will contact you with the exact cost before shipping, and you may cancel for a full refund if you would rather not proceed.

We do not currently ship outside the United States, and we do not ship to P.O. boxes for oversize items.

Sales tax

Listed prices do not include sales tax. Where we are required to collect it, sales tax is calculated from your shipping address and shown as a separate line at checkout before you place your order.

Processing & dispatch

Orders placed before 1:00pm Pacific Time on a business day are usually shipped the same day. Orders placed after that, or on a weekend or federal holiday, ship the next business day. Estimated delivery times run from the point of shipment, not the point of order.

Live plants ship early in the week where possible, so they are not sitting in a facility over a weekend.

Tracking

You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves us. If your tracking has not updated for two business days, please contact us and we will follow up with the carrier.

Missed deliveries

If you are out, the carrier will follow its normal process — a redelivery attempt or a nearby pickup location. Packages are held for a limited period before being returned to us; if that happens we will arrange reshipment, which may incur a further shipping charge.

Delivery timescales are estimates and not guaranteed. See also our returns & refunds policy and terms & conditions.
