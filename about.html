About Fernway

A Home & Garden shop run by people who actually garden.

Who we are

Fernway is an independent Home & Garden retailer based in Portland, Oregon. We started in 2016 out of a single warehouse bay, selling a small, carefully chosen range of pots and tools to local gardeners. We have grown since, but the idea has not changed: stock fewer things, know each of them well, and describe them honestly.

Everything we sell is something we would put in our own yards. If a product will not survive a cold winter outdoors, we say so on the page rather than letting you find out in February.

What we stand for

Three things we will not compromise on

Honesty over hype

Plain descriptions, real measurements and clear care instructions. No invented reviews, no countdown timers, no pressure. Shipping and sales tax are shown before you pay.

Made to be kept

We favor products that can be repaired and maintained — replaceable blades, spare cushions, frost-proof pots — over things designed to be thrown away.

Sourced with care

Our plants are nursery-grown and acclimated before they reach you, and we choose suppliers who share our view on materials and packaging.

Where we are

Our office and warehouse are at 1420 SE Cedar Mill Road, Suite 5, Portland, OR 97214. We are an online shop and do not currently run a public showroom, but you are welcome to reach us by phone at 1 (800) 555-0142 or by email at hello@shopfernway.us during business hours.

Fernway LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Oregon. We ship across the United States.
